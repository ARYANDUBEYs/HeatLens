## WHAT TO SAY WHEN JUDGES ASK...
=============================================================

Q: "Why Azure specifically?"
A: "Azure Maps gives us enterprise-grade geospatial rendering that Leaflet 
cannot match at scale. Azure OpenAI lets non-technical municipal officers 
query satellite data in plain Hindi or English. And Azure Functions make 
our satellite ingestion pipeline globally serverless and auto-scaling."

Q: "Who are your users?"
A: "Municipal corporations, climate researchers, and urban planners. We are 
in conversation with Delhi government climate resilience offices."

Q: "How is this different from Google Earth Engine?"
A: "GEE is a research tool requiring Python expertise. HeatLens is a 
decision-support product. A ward councillor with zero technical background 
can ask in plain language: 'Where do I place cooling centers?' and get 
an immediate, evidence-backed answer."
