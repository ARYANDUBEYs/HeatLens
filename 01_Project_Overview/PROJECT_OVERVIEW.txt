## WHAT IS HEATLENS?
HeatLens is an autonomous geospatial AI platform that:
- Ingests dual-satellite data (Landsat-8/9 TIRS Band 10 @30m + Sentinel-2 MSI NDVI @10m)
- Applies Planck's Law radiometric temperature inversion
- Derives FVC (Fractional Vegetation Cover) and emissivity corrections
- Builds a multi-factor Urban Heat Island (UHI) vulnerability priority index:
    P = 0.35(LST) + 0.25(1-NDVI) + 0.20(AQI) + 0.20(Risk)
- Maps localized thermal disparities (+8°C to +10°C) across 250+ Delhi NCR wards
- Deployed live on Vercel + Hugging Face Spaces (<50ms inference latency)
- Backed by a 6-page IEEE peer-reviewed manuscript

## CORE PROBLEM SOLVED
- 50M+ Delhi NCR residents exposed to extreme urban heat (45°C+ summers)
- Ground-based weather stations are sparse and expensive
- No open-access, ward-level heat vulnerability map exists for Indian cities
- Government lacks real-time tools for climate-adaptive urban planning
